## Clipchamp tips

- **Background behind cards:** duplicate the card image under it and use **Blur fill**. That fills the 9:16 frame with no black bars.
- **Price text:** use bold white with a black outline. Make **−27% / −17% / −32%** red and about twice the size.
- **Captions:** put auto-captions about 75% of the way down the screen so they don't cover the price text. Fix name errors, because auto-captions often turn "Ohtani" into "Otani" or "oh tawny".
- Hook text is on screen from 0:00 with no fade-in.
- Export at **1080p**.
